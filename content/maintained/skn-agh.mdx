I maintain the site for the university body that coordinates AGH's student
scientific associations, built in Gatsby and React with Contentful behind it.

Most of my recent work there has been modernising it for the long term:
making it maintainable by whoever comes next, and making sure it keeps
working after I graduate and hand it over. I also edit content and work
directly with the scientific circles in my domain.
