Tracks calories, protein, fat, and carbs per day. Products are added by
scanning an EAN barcode with the camera against an open products database.
When something is not in there, I create the product or the dish by hand and
enter its macros.

Fitatu wanted the day split into meals, kept pushing AI features I had no use
for, and wrapped the rest in streaks and adverts. None of that helps anyone
eat better.

So there are no meals, no streak, no feed, and no ads. Scan a barcode, log the
macros, move on.
