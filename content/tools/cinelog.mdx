Tracks films and series I have watched, with a watchlist, a watch queue,
ratings, and stats and recaps over time.

I used Trakt until its limits got in the way. Saving more than a hundred
titles sits behind the paywall, and catching up on a series means ticking
episodes one at a time, with no way to mark a whole season. The social half
of it was never why I was there.

So the social half is gone. What is left is closer to a spreadsheet than an
app, which is what this always was: an API underneath, a web version and a
mobile one, and season-level ticking so catching up is one action instead of
one per episode.
