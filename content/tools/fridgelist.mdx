Keeps a stocktake of what is actually in my fridge. Receipts get imported as
JSON rather than typed in by hand, and it generates a shopping list from
what has run out, with a tick-off flow for actually using it in a shop.

The receipt import is the part that makes it worth having — manual entry is
where every other fridge app loses me.
