Keeps a stocktake of what is actually in my fridge. Receipts from Biedronka
import as JSON, things I buy often get saved as regulars, and I tick items
off as I finish them. Once stock runs low it builds the shopping list on its
own.

Nothing on the Play Store did that. They want you to scan every product by
hand to add it, then bury the part you came for under stats nobody reads.

The import is the whole point. If adding food is work, I stop doing it, and
a fridge list that is out of date is worse than no list at all.
