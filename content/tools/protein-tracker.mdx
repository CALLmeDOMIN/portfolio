Tracks calories, protein, fat and carbs per day. Products are added by
scanning an EAN barcode against an open products database. When something is
not in there, I create the product or the dish by hand and enter its macros.

I built this because I did not like using Fitatu. Nutrition trackers tend to
be either subscription-gated or padded with features I have no use for. The
part I actually wanted is small enough to own: scan a barcode, log the
macros, move on.
