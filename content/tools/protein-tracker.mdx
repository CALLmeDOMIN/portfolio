Tracks calories, protein, fat and carbs per day. Products are added by
scanning an EAN barcode against an open products database; when a product
is not in there, I create the product or the dish by hand and enter its
macros.

I built this because I did not like using Fitatu. Nutrition trackers tend to
be either subscription-gated or padded with features I have no use for, and
the part I actually wanted — scan a barcode, log the macros, move on — is
small enough to own.
