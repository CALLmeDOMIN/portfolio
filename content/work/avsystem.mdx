I work on the software an ice cream chain runs on. The Good Lood team is
about fourteen people, and we cover roughly eight systems between us.

The customer-facing ones are a loyalty and q-commerce app with over 500,000
registered users, the backend behind it that also serves point-of-sale and
the Joy Station topping dispenser, and a point-of-sale system handling
millions of transactions a year. The rest are internal: recipe management
that feeds nutrition and allergen data to ERP, tray tracking that follows
containers from production back to return, delivery with driver tracking,
and the in-store screens showing live prices and what flavors are left.

Fourteen people across that many systems means nobody specializes. I work
from mobile through backend to internal tooling, and most of it ends up
touching something physical: a freezer, a delivery van, a tray coming back
from a store.

I joined as an intern over the summer and moved into a full engineering role.
