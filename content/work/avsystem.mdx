I work on the software an ice cream chain runs on. The Good Lood team is
around fourteen people covering roughly eight systems: a loyalty and
q-commerce app with over 500,000 registered users, the backend serving that
app alongside point-of-sale and the Joy Station topping dispenser, a
point-of-sale system handling millions of transactions a year, a recipe
management system that feeds nutrition and allergen data to ERP, tray
tracking that follows containers from production through to return, delivery
with driver tracking, and the in-store screens showing live prices and
flavour availability.

Fourteen people against that many systems means nobody gets to specialise.
The work runs from mobile through backend to internal tooling, and the
interesting part is that most of it touches something physical — a freezer, a
delivery van, a tray coming back from a store.

I joined as an intern over the summer and moved into a full engineering role.
